How to Make Crepes Without Lumps (Easy French Recipe)

Learn how to make crepes with a smooth, lump-free batter: simple ingredients, the right mixing method, resting time and tips to cook thin, golden crepes.

Published on November 10, 2026 · 3 min read

  2. How to make a lump-free batter

  6. Storing leftover crepes

To make crepes without lumps, put the flour in a bowl, make a well, add the eggs, then pour in the milk gradually while whisking from the center outwards. Let the batter rest if you have time, then cook thin layers in a hot, lightly buttered pan. Here is a simple recipe and the tips that make the difference.

Ingredients (about 15 crepes)

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter (plus a little for the pan)
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (optional, for sweet crepes)
  • Optional flavorings: vanilla, orange zest or a splash of rum

For savory crepes, leave out the sugar and flavorings.

How to make a lump-free batter

  1. Put the flour, salt and sugar in a large bowl and make a well in the center.
  2. Add the eggs into the well.
  3. Start whisking in the center, then pour in the milk little by little, gradually incorporating the flour from the edges. A thick, smooth paste should form first.
  4. Once the paste is smooth, add the rest of the milk more quickly while whisking.
  5. Stir in the melted butter.

The secret is in step 3: adding all the milk at once makes the flour form lumps that are hard to dissolve.

If you still have lumps

  • Pass the batter through a sieve, pressing the lumps with a spoon.
  • Or use a hand blender for a few seconds.

Should you let the batter rest?

Resting is not mandatory, but it helps. Leaving the batter covered in the fridge for about an hour lets the flour absorb the liquid and makes crepes easier to cook and less likely to tear. If you are in a hurry, you can cook it straight away.

Before cooking, stir the batter. It should have the consistency of single cream: if it is too thick, add a little milk.

How to cook the crepes

  1. Heat a non-stick pan or crepe pan over medium to high heat.
  2. Lightly grease it with butter using a paper towel.
  3. Pour a small ladle of batter and tilt the pan immediately to spread it in a thin layer.
  4. Cook until the edges turn golden and lift easily, usually about a minute.
  5. Flip with a spatula (or with a flick of the wrist if you are brave) and cook the other side a little less.
  6. Stack the crepes on a plate and cover them to keep them warm and soft.

The first crepe is often a test: do not worry if it is not perfect, adjust the heat and quantity for the next ones.

Filling ideas

Sweet

  • sugar and lemon juice;
  • jam or chocolate spread;
  • sliced banana and honey;
  • stewed apples with cinnamon.

Savory

For savory versions, ham, cheese and an egg make a classic combination. Mushrooms, spinach or smoked salmon also work well.

Storing leftover crepes

Cooked crepes keep in the fridge for a few days, wrapped in film. They also freeze well with baking paper between them. Reheat them in a pan or briefly in the microwave. Leftover batter keeps in a closed container in the fridge for about a day or two.

Crepes are cheap to make, which helps when you want to save money on groceries, and they are a great way to use up eggs before they go off. Children love helping too: see our screen-free activities for kids.

Summary

Make a well in the flour, add the eggs and pour the milk in gradually while whisking to avoid lumps. Add melted butter, let the batter rest if you can, then cook thin crepes in a hot buttered pan for about a minute on each side. Fill them with sweet or savory toppings.
