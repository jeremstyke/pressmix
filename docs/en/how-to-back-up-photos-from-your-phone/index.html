How to Back Up Photos From Your Phone (Android and iPhone)

Do not lose your memories. Here is how to back up photos from your phone with the cloud, a computer or an external drive, on Android and iPhone, step by step.

Published on November 10, 2026 · 3 min read

  7. Summary

The simplest way to back up photos from your phone is to turn on automatic cloud backup: Google Photos on Android, iCloud Photos on iPhone, or another cloud service. For extra safety, also keep a copy on a computer or an external drive. That way, a lost, stolen or broken phone will not take your memories with it.

Why backing up matters

A phone can be lost, stolen, dropped in water or simply stop working. Without a backup, the photos stored only on it are gone. A backup also lets you free up space on your phone, which helps if it is getting slow (see why your phone is slow).

A good rule of thumb used by many professionals is the 3-2-1 rule: three copies of your data, on two different types of storage, with one copy kept elsewhere, such as in the cloud.

Option 1: Back up to the cloud

On Android

Most Android phones come with Google Photos.

  1. Open the Google Photos app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then the backup settings.
  3. Turn on backup and choose the account to use.
  4. Pick the upload quality and decide whether to back up over mobile data or Wi-Fi only.

Google accounts include a limited amount of free storage shared across services. Some phone brands also offer their own cloud.

On iPhone

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Go to iCloud, then Photos.
  3. Turn on Sync this iPhone (called iCloud Photos on some versions).

iCloud includes a small amount of free storage. If your library is large, you will probably need a paid storage plan. Google Photos and other services also work on iPhone.

Check that it works

After turning on backup, keep the phone connected to Wi-Fi and charging for a while, then check on another device or on the web that your photos are there.

Option 2: Copy photos to a computer

Android to computer

Connect the phone with a USB cable, choose file transfer on the phone, then open the phone's storage on your computer. Photos are usually in the DCIM folder. Copy it to your computer.

iPhone to computer

  • On Windows: connect the iPhone, unlock it and tap "Trust", then import photos with the Photos app.
  • On a Mac: open the Photos app with the iPhone connected and click Import.

Option 3: Use an external drive

Copy your photos from the computer to an external hard drive or SSD and store it somewhere safe. Some drives and USB keys also connect directly to phones. Repeat regularly, for example once a month.

Do not forget messaging apps

Photos received in chat apps are not always saved to your gallery automatically. Check each app's settings to save media or use its own chat backup feature.

Keep your backups secure

Your photo library is personal. Protect your cloud account with a strong password and two-factor authentication: see how to create a strong password. And if you upgrade your storage plan, include the cost in your monthly budget.

Summary

Turn on automatic cloud backup with Google Photos, iCloud or another service, and check that your photos really upload. For extra safety, copy them regularly to a computer or external drive, following the 3-2-1 rule. Protect your accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
