How to Fix Lag in Online Games (PC, Console and Mobile)

Lag ruining your matches? Learn the difference between high ping and low FPS, then try these fixes for PC, console and mobile, from Ethernet to graphics.

Published on November 10, 2026 · 3 min read

To fix lag in online games, first find out whether the problem is your connection (high ping) or your device (low FPS). For connection lag, use a wired Ethernet connection, close apps that use bandwidth and pick the closest game server. For low FPS, update your graphics drivers and lower demanding graphics settings. Here is how to diagnose and fix both.

Network lag or low FPS?

"Lag" can describe two different problems:

  • Network lag (high ping): your actions take time to register, players teleport and the game feels delayed. The problem is the connection between you and the game server.
  • Low FPS (frame rate): the image stutters or looks choppy, even in offline modes. The problem is your device's performance.

Most games can display ping and FPS in their settings. Turn these indicators on to see which one drops when the game lags.

Fixes for network lag

Use a wired connection

Wi-Fi is convenient but more sensitive to walls, distance and interference. An Ethernet cable between your PC or console and the router usually gives a more stable connection. If a cable is not possible, move closer to the router or use the 5 GHz band if your router offers it.

Stop other devices from using bandwidth

Large downloads, video streaming, cloud backups and game updates on other devices can slow your connection. Pause downloads while you play and ask others in the household if they are streaming in high quality.

Choose the right server

Playing on a server far away increases ping. In the game settings, select the region closest to you.

Restart your router

A simple restart can clear temporary problems. Unplug the router, wait a short moment, then plug it back in.

Check your router settings

Some routers include a QoS (Quality of Service) or gaming mode that prioritizes game traffic. Updating the router's firmware can also help.

Contact your internet provider

If ping stays high in every game and at every hour, there may be an issue with your line. Run a speed test and contact your provider with the results.

Fixes for low FPS

Update drivers and the game

On PC, update your graphics card drivers from the manufacturer's website or app. Keep the game and your operating system up to date too.

Lower graphics settings

Settings such as shadows, anti-aliasing, resolution scaling and view distance often have a big impact. Lower them one by one and check the FPS counter.

Close background programs

Browsers with many tabs, recording software or launchers running in the background use resources. Close what you do not need.

Check temperatures and power settings

An overheating device can slow itself down. Clean dust from vents, keep consoles in a ventilated spot and, on laptops, play while plugged in with a performance power mode.

Specific tips for mobile gaming

  • Close other apps and turn on any game mode your phone offers.
  • Free up storage space and keep your system updated; see why your phone is slow for a full checklist.
  • Avoid playing while the phone is charging and hot.
  • Prefer a strong Wi-Fi signal over a weak mobile data connection.

When to consider an upgrade

If your hardware is far below the game's minimum requirements, settings will only help so much. Before buying, compare options and plan the expense in your monthly budget rather than buying on impulse.

Summary

Check whether lag comes from high ping or low FPS. For ping, use Ethernet, stop other downloads, choose a nearby server and restart your router. For FPS, update drivers, lower graphics settings, close background programs and keep your device cool. On mobile, free up space and close other apps.
