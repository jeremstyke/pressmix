A phone usually gets slow because its **storage is almost full**, too many apps run in the background or its software is out of date. The good news is that **you rarely need a new phone**: a few free fixes are often enough to make it fast again. Here are 10 solutions, from the quickest to the most drastic.

## Why phones slow down over time

Several things pile up as months go by:

- **Full storage**: phones need free space to run smoothly, and performance can drop when storage is nearly full.
- **Background apps**: some apps keep syncing, tracking location or refreshing content even when you are not using them.
- **Heavier apps and updates**: apps grow bigger every year while your hardware stays the same.
- **An aging battery**: on some phones, a worn battery can lead the system to limit performance to avoid unexpected shutdowns.

## Quick fixes (5 minutes)

### 1. Restart your phone

It sounds too simple, but a restart **clears temporary memory** and stops stuck processes. Many people never turn their phone off for weeks. Restart it at least once a week.

### 2. Close apps that misbehave

You do not need to swipe away every app all the time, since both Android and iOS manage memory well. But if one app freezes or drains the battery, **force close it** and reopen it.

### 3. Update the system and your apps

Updates fix bugs and security holes and can improve performance. Go to **Settings > Software update** on Android or **Settings > General > Software Update** on iPhone, then update your apps from the app store.

## Free up storage

### 4. Check what takes up space

Open **Settings > Storage** (Android) or **Settings > General > iPhone Storage**. You will see which apps, photos and files use the most space. Try to keep a comfortable margin free.

### 5. Delete apps you no longer use

Most of us keep apps we opened once. Uninstall them. On iPhone, the **Offload App** option removes the app but keeps its data in case you need it later.

### 6. Back up photos and videos

Videos are often the biggest space eaters. Back them up to a cloud service or a computer, then remove them from the phone. Also clear **downloaded files** and old media in messaging apps, which can quietly grow very large.

### 7. Clear app caches (Android)

Go to **Settings > Apps**, choose a heavy app such as a browser or social network, then **Storage > Clear cache**. This does not delete your account or your data.

## Lighten the system

### 8. Limit background activity

- On iPhone: **Settings > General > Background App Refresh**, and turn it off for apps that do not need it.
- On Android: check battery settings for apps that use a lot of power in the background and restrict them.

### 9. Reduce animations

Animations make a phone feel slower. On Android, you can reduce them in **Developer options** (tap the build number seven times in "About phone" to unlock them). On iPhone, turn on **Reduce Motion** in the Accessibility settings.

## The last resort

### 10. Factory reset

If nothing works, a factory reset brings the phone back to its original state. **Back up everything first**: photos, contacts, conversations and passwords. Then reinstall only the apps you really use. It takes time, but the difference is often striking.

## When is it time to replace your phone?

Consider a new device if your phone **no longer receives security updates**, if the battery cannot last a day even after replacement, or if it stays slow after a reset. Before buying, compare refurbished models and check your [monthly budget](/en/how-to-make-a-monthly-budget/) so the purchase does not catch you off guard.

If games are what feel slow, read our guide on [how to fix lag in online games](/en/how-to-fix-lag-in-online-games/), since the problem is not always the phone itself.

## Summary

A slow phone is usually caused by full storage, background apps or outdated software. Restart it, update it, free up space and limit background activity before thinking about a reset. Most phones get a real speed boost from these simple steps, without spending anything.
